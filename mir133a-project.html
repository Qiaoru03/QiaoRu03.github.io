Research Experience | Molecular Biology

Spatio-temporal expression of miR-133a-5p in Siniperca chuatsi.

This project characterized the spatial and developmental expression pattern of miR-133a-5p in Siniperca chuatsi, providing a molecular view of how this muscle-associated microRNA varies across adult tissues and embryonic stages.

Selected work

Two representative analyses summarize the spatial distribution and embryonic-stage expression pattern of miR-133a-5p.

01 Tissue distribution

Tissue distribution of miR-133a-5p in S. chuatsi

miR-133a-5p showed marked tissue specificity, with the strongest expression in slow-twitch muscle and relatively high expression in heart and several fast-twitch muscle regions, while non-muscle tissues showed substantially lower levels.

Selected result: tissue-specific expression profiling by qPCR.

02 Embryonic-stage expression

Embryonic-stage expression of miR-133a-5p in S. chuatsi

miR-133a-5p expression was highest at the earliest embryonic stages and declined markedly as development progressed, indicating strong temporal regulation during embryogenesis.

Selected result: developmental expression profiling across embryonic stages.

Publication
