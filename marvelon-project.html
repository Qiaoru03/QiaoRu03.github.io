Research Experience | Tumor Immunity

Marvelon suppresses MC38 tumor growth and promotes antitumor immunity.

Marvelon suppresses MC38 tumor growth and promotes antitumor immunity. This project investigated the antitumor effects of Marvelon using the MC38 mouse tumor model and immune functional analysis, linking tumor growth control with changes in antitumor immune activity.

Molecular Immunology · 2025 Fourth author Tumor immunity
Research framework

From tumor growth to antitumor immune function

01 · In vivo model

MC38 tumor model

Evaluate tumor growth under Marvelon treatment.

02 · Immune response

Tumor-infiltrating CD8⁺ T cells

Assess functional changes in antitumor T-cell responses.

03 · In vitro support

Tumor & immune-cell assays

Use complementary in vitro experiments to interpret treatment effects.

My role

I participated in the in vivo MC38 tumor model, functional analysis of tumor-infiltrating CD8⁺ T cells, and related in vitro tumor-cell and immune-cell experiments.

Selected work

My experimental contribution spanned three connected parts: tumor growth assessment, tumor-infiltrating CD8⁺ T-cell function, and complementary in vitro assays.

01 In vivo tumor model

MC38 tumor-model work and longitudinal growth assessment

Marvelon treatment was associated with lower MC38 tumor volume than the control group, with a significant difference at day 23.

My contribution: tumor-volume monitoring and calculation.

02 Tumor-infiltrating CD8⁺ T cells

Functional analysis of tumor-infiltrating CD8⁺ T cells

The Marvelon group showed a higher proportion of IFN-γ-positive cells among tumor-infiltrating CD8⁺ T cells than the control group.

My contribution: tumor-infiltrating immune-cell functional profiling.

03 In vitro experiments

MC38 tumor-cell and immune-cell related assays

MC38 cell viability did not differ significantly across the tested desogestrel concentrations. CFSE distributions were measured across ethinylestradiol concentrations to examine immune-cell proliferation-related responses.

My contribution: in vitro tumor-cell and immune-cell related experiments.

Publication

This work resulted in a fourth-author publication in Molecular Immunology.
